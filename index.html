We start with what is already there: a prevailing tropical wind, intense solar azimuth, the way water marks porous stone. Architecture is an act of calibrated attention.

01 / Observe

Climate telemetry, solar radiation audits, and material microclimates native to Sulawesi.

02 / Compute

Generative spatial algorithms, daylight factor optimization (SNI), and structural efficiency.

03 / Craft

Precision detailing from digital BIM geometry to tactile construction thresholds.
