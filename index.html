The Tropical Daylighting Paradox.

In equatorial Sulawesi, unfiltered sunlight provides abundant lumen—until afternoon glare forces occupants to pull blinds and switch on artificial lighting. In the Mattoanging Community Hub master research, Heru Ardiansyah engineered an adaptive kinetic building envelope governed by surrogate machine learning (ANN & XGBoost). Louvers continuously track equatorial sun vectors, bouncing daylight deep across 0.75m desk planes while eliminating direct solar heat.

site first.

GradiEnt starts with what is already there: a prevailing tropical wind, intense solar azimuth, the way water marks porous stone. Architecture is an act of calibrated attention.

01 / Microclimate Telemetry

Solar azimuth mapping, EPW wind velocity vectors, and humidity audits before sketching the first line.

02 / Daylighting Computation

Surrogate AI models calibrated to SNI 03-6197 and ANSI/IES LM-83-23 to eliminate glare and mechanical overcooling.

03 / Tactile Craftsmanship

Translating digital geometry into marine-grade AA6063 aluminium, local porous brick, and enduring timber thresholds.
